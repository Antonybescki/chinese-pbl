The Central Axis

One road. Twelve stops. Twelve teachers.

Beijing is built on a single line that runs north to south through the middle of the city. Most of the famous places sit on it or close beside it, so this page walks that road, one stop at a time.

Roll call

Meet the class. Tap a teacher to hear them.

Five teachers speak in their own anime voices. The other seven say their Chinese word out loud.

欢迎

01欢迎Welcome

Welcome to the northern capital

  • Beijing means "northern capital".
  • About 22 million people live here.
欢迎来到北京
Huānyíng lái dào Běijīng
Welcome to Beijing
长城

02长城Great Wall

The Great Wall, a dragon of stone

  • All the walls add up to 21,196 km.
  • The Ming dynasty rebuilt the parts near Beijing.
不到长城非好汉
Bú dào Chángchéng fēi hǎohàn
He who has not climbed the Great Wall is no hero
故宫

03故宫Forbidden City

Inside the Forbidden City

  • Built from 1406 to 1420, with about 980 buildings.
  • 24 emperors lived here.
故宫很大
Gùgōng hěn dà
The Forbidden City is big
天坛

04天坛Temple of Heaven

Temple of Heaven, above the clouds

  • The round Hall of Prayer was built without nails.
  • It stands 38 metres high.
我想去天坛
Wǒ xiǎng qù Tiāntán
I want to go to the Temple of Heaven
胡同

05胡同Hutongs

Old Beijing, lane by lane

  • Narrow brick lanes with courtyard homes called siheyuan.
  • Most date from the Yuan, Ming and Qing dynasties.
这条胡同很老
Zhè tiáo hútòng hěn lǎo
This lane is very old
美食

06美食Food

The flavour of Beijing

  • Peking duck, zhajiang noodles, jianbing, dumplings and hotpot.
  • Wrap the duck in a thin pancake with sweet sauce.
我想吃北京烤鸭
Wǒ xiǎng chī Běijīng kǎoyā
I want to eat Peking duck
现代

07现代Modern Beijing

Tomorrow's capital

  • China Zun is 528 metres tall.
  • Daxing Airport is shaped like a starfish.
北京很现代
Běijīng hěn xiàndài
Beijing is very modern
交通

08交通Transit

Moving through the city

  • The subway has more than 800 km of track.
  • The train to Shanghai takes about 4.5 hours.
地铁在哪儿?
Dìtiě zài nǎr?
Where is the subway?
公园

09公园Parks and Hills

Beyond the walls: parks, hills and tombs

  • Fragrant Hills turn red in autumn.
  • The Ming Tombs hold 13 emperors.
秋天的香山很美
Qiūtiān de Xiāngshān hěn měi
Fragrant Hills are beautiful in autumn
中文

10中文Language

Speak like a local

  • Mandarin has 4 tones plus a light one.
  • Say 你好 (nǐ hǎo) for hello.
你好,谢谢,再见
Nǐ hǎo, xièxie, zàijiàn
Hello, thank you, goodbye
四季

11四季Seasons and Festivals

When to go

  • The best time to visit is September and October.
  • Avoid the first week of October, when National Day crowds arrive.
秋天最好
Qiūtiān zuì hǎo
Autumn is the best
三日

12三日Three Days

Three perfect days

  • Day 1, the imperial heart. Day 2, the Great Wall.
  • Day 3, local life: hutongs, tea and the lake.
我们玩三天
Wǒmen wán sān tiān
We will spend three days
谢谢

Thank you. Zàijiàn.
