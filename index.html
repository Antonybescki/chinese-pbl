关于中文

About the Chinese language

Mandarin Chinese (普通话, Pǔtōnghuà) is the official language of China and the most widely spoken first language in the world.

900M+

Native speakers

More people speak Mandarin as their mother tongue than any other language.

3,000+

Years of writing

Chinese characters trace back to oracle bone inscriptions of the Shang dynasty.

4 + 1

Tones

Four main tones and one neutral tone change the meaning of a syllable.

6

UN languages

Chinese is one of the six official languages of the United Nations.

汉字 · Hànzì

Characters are the written form. Each one stands for a syllable with a meaning, not a letter.

拼音 · Pīnyīn

Pinyin writes Chinese sounds with the Roman alphabet, so beginners can read and pronounce words.

声调 · Shēngdiào

Tones are the pitch pattern of a syllable. The marks above pinyin vowels show which tone to use.

Numbers 1 – 10

Once you know one to ten, bigger numbers are easy: 11 is “ten-one” (十一) and 20 is “two-ten” (二十).

中国文化

Chinese culture

Language and culture go together. These festivals and traditions are part of daily life in China.

春节
Chūnjié · Spring Festival

Chinese New Year. Families reunite, share dumplings and give red envelopes (红包).

元宵节
Yuánxiāojié · Lantern Festival

The 15th day of the new year, celebrated with glowing lanterns and sweet rice balls.

端午节
Duānwǔjié · Dragon Boat Festival

Dragon boat races and sticky rice dumplings (粽子) in memory of the poet Qu Yuan.

中秋节
Zhōngqiūjié · Mid-Autumn Festival

Families admire the full moon together and eat mooncakes (月饼).

十二生肖 · The zodiac

Twelve animals — rat, ox, tiger, rabbit, dragon, snake, horse, goat, monkey, rooster, dog and pig — each rule one year in turn.

茶 · Tea

China is the home of tea. Offering a cup of tea to a guest is a sign of respect and welcome.

书法 · Calligraphy

Writing characters with brush and ink is treated as an art form, not only as handwriting.
